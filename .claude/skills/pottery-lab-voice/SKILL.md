# Pottery Lab voice

Follow the unslop rules, then this. The full brand kit is `docs/brand-kit.md`.
### Unnatural metaphors

- **Forced craft or object metaphors.** "Taped in", "taped into your page", "tape it in", "stitched together", "baked in", "woven into". They sound cute in a brainstorm and odd on a screen. Say where the thing actually goes: "in your scrapbook", "on your page", "saved to your list".

## Brand voice: Pottery Lab

When writing anything for Pottery Lab (the app, its site, emails, decks or outreach), also follow the brand kit in the repo at `docs/brand-kit.md`. The short version:

- **Tagline, word for word:** "Your co-host for every plan." Supporting line: "Potto brainstorms the idea, finds the time that works for everyone, designs the invite, chases the replies and keeps the group going." Under it: "We've been the planner. This is the tool we wished we had."
- **Story order:** host your people, keep every event in your scrapbook, get found by strangers, get booked by brands. Hosts first, guests second, brands last. Never call it a marketplace.
- **Voice:** warm, plain, a little playful. One light touch per screen. Potto (the clay pot helper) speaks at most once per screen, in first person, and never invents facts.
- **One word per thing:** host, guests, brands, spots, your page, scrapbook (in product copy), event or experience. Replies are "I'm in / Maybe / Can't go". Approval is "Ask to join / Let in / Not this time". Prices read "$X a person".
- **Never use:** "taped in", "marketplace", "seamless", "effortless", "unlock", "elevate", "curated", "magical", "attendees", "seats", "per person", "publish" (in host copy), "say yes" (use "lets you in").
- **Honesty:** no invented stats, testimonials, logos, press or promises. Never print a fee or take-rate percentage.
