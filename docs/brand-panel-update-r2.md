# Brand product update for the panel, round 2

Thank you for the second set of scores. Here is what changed.

**Ease of use.** If a host cancels close to the date, the event owner gets one alert. We show up to three comparable hosts in the same city, date window, size and price band, and each one lists what differs. One tap rebooks with the same brief and shows the price difference. A note to guests is drafted ("Change of plan, same evening"), and the brand picks a refund or credit. When choices are thin, the page says so. In pilot mode, "Run the drill" walks through all of this without cancelling anything, and records how long recovery took. A company can now hold several teams that share approval rules, brand rules and invoicing. For account dinners, the marketer picks accounts and a budget, each account owner approves their own customers, and the host approves the mix. The approval page lists the host's standard price, any custom work and the prep hours on separate lines, and flags repeated unpaid revisions.

**Usefulness.** Reports show follow-up speed for each account owner, an event tier with a paragraph you can forward, and whether matched guests met again (with a light thread and a nudge). They also include RSVP preferences (the brand only sees totals), a rebooking rule set before invites go out, an open "What did you take away?" question, and two changes for next time.

**Innovation.** Recommendations are now lift against a baseline, with both sample sizes and the source of each figure. There is a library of 13 formats, and room rules are standard fields in every brief.

**Durability.** Book again holds seats for returning guests and keeps matched pairs together. Repeat bookings with the same host pay half the platform fee. Hosts can download their followers. Guests who opt in hear about similar hosts.

## Evidence plan

**Before launch:** founding hosts' signed dates, paid pilots, earnings per hour against their own events, the share of their seats filled by followers, and whether they'd send their next brand inquiry through us. Design partners pre-sign a second booking that triggers when the rule is met. A crossover test between hosts.

**After launch, on /admin/brand-metrics:** customer dinners 40%+ rebook within 90 days. Team events 50%+ rebook within 12 months, with 30%+ showing a leading signal within 60 days. Community guests 25%+ return within 60 days. Second-booking seats 20%+ from first-event guests. 30%+ of rebookers try a different host. 15%+ of guests attend two hosts within 90 days. 60%+ of the first host cohort get a second paid booking within 120 days. Plus the on-platform rate for repeat pairs. Each bar shows its denominator and a pass or fail, by launch cohort, with design partners reported separately.
