# Brand product update for the panel, round 3

Thank you for the third set of scores. Here is what changed.

**Ease of use.** The note to guests says "same evening" only when the date and time stay the same. Otherwise it gives the new date and time, and any change to the format or access. The brand can hold a replacement host for 12, 24 or 48 hours, and both sides see a clock. The hold ends on its own if the brand doesn't commit in time, or if fewer guests can come than the number the owner set. If the date, time or access changes, each guest gets a link to reconfirm. The owner sees the count, target accounts on their own line, and the new price and deposit before committing. If nothing works, "Cancel the event" gives a refund or credit and drafts the note. A drill counts as an operations test. A recovery counts as a success only once the original target guests come.

**Innovation.** We say "lift" only when both groups match on invite mix, invites sent, spend per guest, city and goal, checked before the event. Anything else is an "Observed difference".

**Durability.** After each event, the brand and the host are each asked if they booked each other outside Pottery Lab. Those bookings count in the on-platform rate. We also report host earnings per prep hour on repeat bookings, our margin after replacement costs, and the first two ordinary host cohorts.
