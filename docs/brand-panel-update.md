# Brand product update for the panel

Thank you for the scores. Here is what changed, in the order you asked.

**Ease of use.** Home and Discover now open on Quick book. You enter a date, the group size, a budget per person, a city and a goal (customer dinner, team bonding, community or workshop), and you see three hosts. Each card shows the full total, the dollar amount at risk on each date, accessibility notes and whether the date is open. You can book one event without setting up a campaign. Each booking has one owner, one status, one approval line and one timeline, plus a read-only summary link for finance and sales. Hosts confirm capacity and answer change requests in one place, and guests get a short note from a named person with a one-tap I'm in. When an event works, "Turn this into a series" copies its format, guest segments, approvals and brief into a campaign.

**Usefulness.** Before booking, the brand and the host agree on a brief: the goal, brand seats vs host seats, creative control, who invites, photo rules, follow-up rules and what guests do together. The brief shows up in the run of show. Reports now record who supplied each seat, which target accounts were invited and which came, an opt-in survey, connections that open only when both guests say yes, and follow-up tasks with an owner and a due date. Meetings and opportunities are counted separately from attendance. Every number shows its denominator. Each goal has its own metrics. HubSpot and Salesforce sync is in beta, with a dry run and consent-only records.

**Innovation.** Recommendations based on outcomes always show the sample size and a confidence label. Experience pages have a "What it's like" section with recap photos and quotes that guests agreed to. There is also a pilot mode that compares the first run against your usual process.

**Durability.** Hosts can see their earnings, repeat brands, how many seats their followers filled, and which bookings came from their own audience. Guests can follow a host on their own, separately from consenting to the brand. Brands get a "Your hosts" list and a Book again button. An admin page tracks host cohorts and rebooking.
