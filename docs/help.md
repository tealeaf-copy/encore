# Pottery Lab help

Potto's help guide. Potto answers from this page only, so keep every line true to the product. Each `##` section is one answer: the first lines are what Potto says, so lead with the steps. `Keywords:` and `Questions:` help Potto find it (questions are split by `|`). Links become one-tap buttons under the answer: a page link, `/@{handle}` (the host's own page), or an app action: `#do:share` (open the share sheet), `#do:copy` (copy the invite), `#do:preview` (see what guests see), `#do:guests` (open the guest list), `#do:open` (open the event), `#do:new` (start a new event).

## Signing up

Keywords: sign up, signup, start, create account, register, new account, start your page
Questions: How do I sign up? | How do I make an account? | How do I start my page?
Go to Start your page, add your name, email, city and a password, then tap Create my page. Hosting friends is free. You land on your home screen, ready to make your first event.
[Start your page](/signup?mode=friends)

## Host codes and invite-only

Keywords: host code, invite-only, invite code, need a code, no code, get a code, ABCD, your turn
Questions: I don't have a host code. How do I get one? | How do I get a host code? | My host code doesn't work
Hosting is invite-only for now, so you need a host code from a host you know. Been a guest at an event here? Tap Your turn? on that invite. You don't need a code. No code? Tap join the waitlist on the sign-up form. Type your code in the Host code box; it says who invited you when it works. A code that doesn't work may be used up, so ask your host for a fresh one.
[Join the waitlist](/signup#ask)

Keywords: waitlist, wait list, waiting list, on the waitlist, room, when can i host
Questions: I'm on the waitlist. What now? | When will I get off the waitlist?
On the waitlist, we email you when there's room. You can skip it: any host you know can send you a host code, then you sign up with it.
[I have a host code](/signup)

Keywords: give code, my codes, my host codes, give host code, invite a friend to host, share code, waitlist people, codes for friends

Every host gets host codes for friends. Open one of your events, tap Your followers in Potto's panel, and they're under Your host codes. People waiting for a code show there too. Host an event with 3 or more guests and you get 10 more.

## Password rules

Keywords: password rules, characters, too short, strong password, length
Questions: What are the password rules? | How long does my password need to be?
Your password needs 10 or more characters. Anything works: words, spaces, numbers. Pick one you don't use anywhere else.

Questions: I can't sign in | It says my email and password don't match
Sign in with the email and password you signed up with. "That email and password don't match" means one of them is off, so try Forgot your password. After 10 wrong tries we pause sign-in for a while; take a breather and try again later.

## What a page is

Keywords: what is a page, host page, my page, @, public page
Questions: What is a page? | Where is my page?
Your page is your public home, at a link that ends in /@yourhandle. It shows what you host next, your past events, kudos from guests and, if you want, a shop. People follow it with just an email, so they hear first next time. Tap My page in the menu to see it.
[Open my page](/@{handle})

## Choosing or changing your handle

Keywords: handle, username, change handle, rename, slug, @name, page link
Questions: How do I choose or change my handle? | Can I change my page link?
Your handle is the end of your page link, 2 to 30 lowercase letters, numbers or dashes. If you typed a club or group name at sign-up, it became your handle. Otherwise open an event, tap Your followers in Potto's panel, pick a handle and tap Create my host page. There's no button to change it later yet, so talk to a person and we'll change it for you.

Questions: How do I edit my event? | How do I change the date or time? | I put the wrong place
On Home, tap the event, then tap Edit details. Change the name, date, time, place, price or spots and tap Save details. The invite link stays the same, and guests see the change when they open it. To change who can come or who sees it, tap the guest rules line instead.

## Cancelling an event

Keywords: cancel event, call off, called off, cancel, postpone, not happening, rain out
Questions: How do I cancel my event? | I need to call off my event | Can I postpone my event?
There's no Cancel button yet. Tell your guests in your group chat first. On the event, turn off Remind guests the day before so no reminder goes out, and set it to Link only in Guest rules so new people can't find it. Then talk to a person and we'll call it off for you. To move it instead, tap Edit details and change the date.

Events are free unless you add a price. Open the event, tap Edit details, and type a price in Price per spot, or Pay what you like; leave it empty for free. Before you share a paid event, the last step asks how guests pay, so add a payment link or pick At the door.

## How guests pay

Keywords: pay, payment, pay link, venmo, paypal, cash app, paid spots, money, at the door, collect
Questions: How do guests pay? | How do guests pay me? | Does Pottery Lab take the money?
Add a payment link (Venmo, PayPal.me, Cash App or any https link) when the wizard asks How do guests pay?, or later in Edit details under Payment link. Venmo, PayPal.me and Cash App links get the amount filled in for guests. Pottery Lab doesn't take the money; it goes straight to you. Guests can tap I paid, and you confirm it on their name.

## Weekly, monthly and courses

Keywords: weekly, monthly, weekly events, monthly events, repeating events, repeat, every week, every month, recurring, run club, book club, next one, next date, course, sessions, class pass, drop-in, drop in
Questions: How do weekly events work? | Does my monthly book club make the next one? | How do I set up a 6 week course? | How do I make my event repeat?
Open your event and set Repeats, under More options, to Every week, Every two weeks or Every month. The morning after each date, the next one appears with the same title, place, price, spots and look, and guests on last week's link see the next date. To stop, set a Last date or set Repeats to Just once. For a course, tell Potto "6-week pottery course" or "8 sessions": it's one event with every date on it. Say "$20 a class or $70 for 4 classes" for a pass.

Events that repeat, or share a name, are a series, and each date gets a number like "Hack night #14". Ran it before Pottery Lab? A guest replies Maybe, then opens I'm in if a friend is and types a friend's name. When someone with that name replies I'm in, the Maybe turns into I'm in, or the waitlist if it's full. The friend is never told.

## Invite friends

Keywords: invite friends, come with me, bring a friend, hold a spot, friend's spot, plus one link, together
Questions: How does Invite friends work? | How do I bring a friend? | Can I hold a spot for a friend?
After you reply I'm in, tap Invite friends to make a link for one friend. It opens the share sheet: the link, a flyer and a QR code. On most events their spot is held for 48 hours. On pairs and groups they join yours. Ask to join events still ask: the link holds nothing. Once you're both in, Who's coming says "Mia and Jo are in".

## Text with Potto

Keywords: text potto, potto by text, sms, whatsapp, text message, texting, stop texts, unsubscribe, quiet hours
Questions: Can I text Potto? | How do I use Potto by text or WhatsApp? | How do I stop texts from Potto?
In Settings, add your mobile number, confirm it with the code we text you, then turn on Text with Potto. Text things like "my plans", "I'm in", "where is it" or "what's on this weekend". ## The shop and pre-orders

Keywords: shop, sell, pre-order, preorder, orders, pickup, cake, merch, products, order form
Questions: How do I set up my shop? | How do pre-orders work? | Where do I see orders?
On your page, tap 🛍 Shop (on a computer it says Shop and bookings). In the Shop tab, list what you're selling with a price and how many you can make, and add pickup times. People order with a pickup time. In the Orders tab you see each order, set it to Paid, Ready or Picked up, and tap Text everyone when it's ready. For a pre-order on one event, tell Potto what you're selling, the price, how many and when to order by: guests pick items when they reply, and orders close on the order-by day or when it sells out.

## Following a host

Keywords: follow, following, followers, unfollow, hear first
Questions: How do I follow a host? | Who follows me?
On a host's page, tap Follow. It takes a free account, made right there, and you hear first when they host next. The bell next to Following turns those notices on or off; tap Following to unfollow. Hosts you follow are on your Me sheet. Who follows you is private: only you see the list, everyone else sees a count. Your own followers are under Your followers in Potto's panel on any of your events.

## Fan clubs

Keywords: fan club, regulars, members, join a club, members only, early access, first dibs, host updates, member badge, leave a club
Questions: What is a fan club? | How do I start a fan club? | How do I join a fan club? | What do members get?
A fan club is for a host's regulars. Hosts start it from the Fan club card on their own page: pick a name (it starts as "Your name's Regulars") and a one-line pitch. Members get first dibs on new events and drops (24 hours before everyone else unless the host picks another window), events set to Fan club only, a members' group chat, and short updates from the host. Joining is free: tap Join on the Fan club card on the host's page. Leave anytime from the same card, and turn off a club's notices from the link in any club email. Hosts see members by first name and who comes most, and can export the list as CSV.

## Free at launch

Keywords: is it free, free at launch, cost, membership fee, paid plan, in-app purchase, app store, supporter tiers, pledge
Questions: Is Pottery Lab free? | Do fan clubs cost money? | Why can't I pledge in the app?
Pottery Lab is free at launch: following, fan clubs and members-only events cost nothing, and there's no paid plan or in-app purchase. The older supporter tiers, pledges and tip links are web only. App Store rules on paying creators mean the iPhone and Android apps never show them, so open the host's page in a browser to chip in.

Guests' emails and phone numbers are only for you; other guests and the public never see them. Exact home addresses go only to guests who are in. Imported past events keep only the neighborhood or city.

## Streaks, rest passes and repair

Keywords: streak, streaks, rest pass, rest month, repair, lost my streak, streak ended, weekly streak, monthly streak, show my streak
Questions: How do streaks work? | What is a rest pass? | How do I repair my streak? | Can I count my streak weekly? | Who can see my streak?
Your streak counts the months in a row you hosted something, any size. Weekly clubs can count weeks instead in Settings, under Hosting. Your first event earns a rest pass, then one every 3 hosted months (or 4 hosted weeks), and you can hold 2. A quiet month uses one by itself. With no pass left, the streak pauses: host anything within 14 days, even a coffee, and it's back. Only you see your streak unless you turn on Show my streak on my page.
[Open Settings](/settings#hosting)

## Small moves and hosting points

Keywords: small move, today's small move, hosting points, warmth, warmth points, monthly goal, goal ring, points
Questions: What is today's small move? | What are hosting points? | How do I change my monthly goal?
Today's small move on Home is one thing that takes a minute or two: send a reminder, thank your guests, post a photo, answer a reply, share a feeler, invite one new person or set the next date. Tap Done when it's done. Hosting points count hosting effort: 10 for each event held, 2 for each guest welcomed, 3 for each would go again, and 1 for each small move (up to 20 a month). Pick a monthly goal of 15, 30 or 60 in Settings.

## Circles

Keywords: circles, circle, league, leagues, ranking, move up, table, kitchen table, leave circle
Questions: What are Circles? | How do I join a circle? | How do I leave my circle? | Can I be moved down?
Circles are off unless you join. Join from Home, or open Circles. You're grouped with about 10 hosts near your size, in your city when there are enough, and ranked on effort (events held, guests welcomed, would go again, small moves), not followers. At the end of each month (or week), the top three move up a table. Everyone else stays. Nobody is moved down, and only your circle sees the board. Leave any time from the same page.

## Notes from Potto

Keywords: notifications from potto, nudges, reminders from potto, too many notifications, quiet hours, stop potto
Questions: Why did Potto message me? | How do I get fewer notes? | What are quiet hours?
Potto sends at most 2 notes a week, at the time you usually plan, and never in quiet hours (9pm to 9am unless you change it). There are four kinds: before an event, after an event, streak heads-ups, and a nudge when it's been a while. Turn any of them off in Settings, under Hosting.

## Kudos and comments on the feed

Keywords: kudos, comments, comment, reply, who gave kudos, hide my name, a guest, feed privacy, post page
Questions: Who can see my comments? | How do I hide my name on kudos? | Why does it say A guest?
Kudos and comments sit right on each feed card, for everyone who can see that card, signed in or not. A private or members-only event keeps its own limits. Tap View all comments or the line of faces to open the post, with every comment, replies and everyone who gave kudos. To choose who sees your name next to your kudos and comments, go to Settings, Your name on the feed: Everyone, People I follow or Only hosts. Anyone else sees "A guest". Hosts always see who commented on their own posts. If you block someone, their kudos and comments are gone for you.
