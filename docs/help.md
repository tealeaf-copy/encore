# Pottery Lab help

Potto's help guide. Potto answers from this page only, so keep every line true to the product. Each `##` section is one answer. The `Keywords:` line helps Potto find it when there's no AI key. Links become buttons under the answer.

## Signing up

Keywords: sign up, signup, start, account, create, register, join, new
Go to Start your page, add your name, email, city and a password, then tap Create my page. Hosting friends is free. You land on your home screen, ready to make your first event.
[Start your page](/signup?mode=friends)

## Host codes and invite-only

Keywords: code, host code, invite, invite-only, invite code, need a code, no code, ABCD, guest, your turn
Hosting is invite-only for now, so you need a host code from a host you know. Been a guest at an event here? Tap Your turn? on that invite. You don't need a code. No code? Tap join the waitlist on the sign-up form. Type your code in the Host code box; it says who invited you when it works. A code that doesn't work may be used up, so ask your host for a fresh one.
[Join the waitlist](/signup#ask)

## The waitlist

Keywords: waitlist, wait list, waiting list, on the waitlist, room, when can i host
On the waitlist, we email you when there's room. You can skip it: any host you know can send you a host code, then you sign up with it.
[I have a host code](/signup)

## Giving out host codes

Keywords: give code, my codes, friends host, invite a friend, share code, waitlist people
Every host gets host codes for friends. Open one of your events, tap Your followers in Potto's panel, and they're under Your host codes. People waiting for a code show there too. Host an event with 3 or more guests and you get 10 more.

## Email already in use

Keywords: email taken, already have an account, already in use, exists, used, conflict, existing email
"You already have an account" means that email is already signed up. Sign in instead, or use Forgot your password if you don't remember it. If you think someone else used your email, talk to a person.
[Sign in](/login) [Forgot your password?](/forgot)

## Password rules

Keywords: password, characters, short, rules, strong, length
Your password needs 10 or more characters. Anything works: words, spaces, numbers. Pick one you don't use anywhere else.

## Signing in

Keywords: sign in, log in, login, can't sign in, wrong password, locked, too many tries
Sign in with the email and password you signed up with. "That email and password don't match" means one of them is off, so try Forgot your password. After 10 wrong tries we pause sign-in for a while; take a breather and try again later.
[Sign in](/login)

## Reset email didn't arrive

Keywords: forgot, reset, reset email, no email, didn't get, didn't arrive, spam, link expired
Tap Forgot your password on the sign-in page and enter your email. The link works once and expires in an hour, so check spam and use the newest one. If it says we can't send reset emails right now, email isn't set up on our side yet, so talk to a person and we'll get you back in.
[Forgot your password?](/forgot)

## Sign in with Google

Keywords: google, gmail, sign in with google, oauth, sso, single sign-on, okta, microsoft
There's no Sign in with Google button. You sign in with your email and a password. Teams whose company set up single sign-on can use Sign in with SSO on the sign-in page. Connecting Gmail in brand onboarding is only for reading quotes, not for signing in.
[Sign in with SSO](/sso/start)

## What a page is

Keywords: page, host page, my page, profile, storefront, what is a page, @
Your page is your public home, at a link that ends in /@yourhandle. It shows what you host next, your past events, kudos from guests and, if you want, a shop. People follow it with just an email, so they hear first next time. Tap My page in the menu to see it.

## Choosing or changing your handle

Keywords: handle, username, link, url, @name, change handle, rename, slug
Your handle is the end of your page link, 2 to 30 lowercase letters, numbers or dashes. If you typed a club or group name at sign-up, it became your handle. Otherwise open an event, tap Your followers in Potto's panel, pick a handle and tap Create my host page. There's no button to change it later yet, so talk to a person and we'll change it for you.

## Setting up your page

Keywords: setup, set up page, name, photo, face, picture, line about you, bio, tagline, look, color, font, cover, background, edit page, make it yours
Open your page and tap Edit page. In Make it yours you pick a color, font, background, cover, a photo or a drawn face, a tagline and links, then tap Save. Add a name, a line about you and a face or photo; Discover only lists hosts whose page has all three.

## Change my page look

Keywords: look, page look, change my page, change look, theme, style, colors, design, font, background, cover photo
On your page, tap Edit page. Change Color, Font, Background, Cover or Buttons and you'll see it right away. Tap Save when it looks right.

## Creating your first event

Keywords: first event, new event, create event, make event, one sentence, plan, start event, wizard, Potto
Tap New event and tell Potto the plan in one sentence, like you'd text a friend: "Dumpling night at my place, Saturday 7pm, about 8 people". Potto fills in the name, time and place. Then check Name, time and place, Who can come and Pick a look, and tap Share invite on the last step.
[New event](/?app=1&new=1#studio)

## Ask to join or open

Keywords: ask to join, approve, approval, let in, open, anyone with the link, who can come, private
On the Who can come step pick Anyone with the link (guests tap I'm in) or I let each guest in (guests ask to join and you let them in). Requests show on your event with Let in and Not this time. To change it later, open the event and tap the guest rules line to open Guest rules.

## Free or paid

Keywords: free, paid, price, charge, cost, money, ticket, pay what you like, chip in
Events are free unless you add a price. Open the event, tap Edit details, and type a price in Price per spot, or Pay what you like; leave it empty for free. Before you share a paid event, the last step asks how guests pay, so add a payment link or pick At the door.

## How guests pay

Keywords: pay, payment, pay link, venmo, paypal, cash app, paid spots, money, at the door, collect
Add a payment link (Venmo, PayPal.me, Cash App or any https link) when the wizard asks How do guests pay?, or later in Edit details under Payment link. Venmo, PayPal.me and Cash App links get the amount filled in for guests. Pottery Lab doesn't take the money; it goes straight to you. Guests can tap I paid, and you confirm it on their name.

## Sending the pay link later

Keywords: send pay link, said yes before link, unpaid, who paid, says paid
If guests said yes before you added a pay link, your event shows Send the pay link. It gives you one message to copy into your group chat, or to send to each guest.

## Sharing your invite

Keywords: share, send, invite, link, copy invite, text, whatsapp, instagram, group chat, preview
After Share invite, tap Copy invite and paste it into a text, group chat or story. The link shows your title, date and cover when you paste it. Tap Preview to see what guests see. To share again later, open the event and use Copy invite.

## Who can see your event

Keywords: visibility, who can see, link only, discover, public, private, on my page, hidden
On the last step pick Link only (only people you send it to), On my page (followers see it too) or On my page and Discover (new people nearby can find it). Events at your home start as Link only. To change it later, open the event and tap the guest rules line.

## What guests see

Keywords: guest view, what guests see, invite page, address, privacy, guest list, who's coming
Guests see the title, date, time, place, price and what to bring. For a get-together at home, guests see the neighborhood, and the exact address only once they're in. The guest list shows only guests who chose to show their name, and you can hide it in Guest rules. Guests never see other guests' emails or phone numbers.

## Guest replies

Keywords: replies, rsvp, responses, who's coming, guests, guest list, see guests, maybe, can't make it
Open your event to see who's in, who might come and who can't. Guests can change their reply from the same link. When you let each guest in, new requests show with Let in and Not this time.

## Reminders

Keywords: reminder, reminders, remind, send reminders, remind guests, day before, texts, emails to guests
Potto reminds guests who said yes the day before, by email, and by text when they gave a phone and texting is set up. The Remind guests the day before switch on your event is on by default; turn it off for that event if you'd rather not. You get a note in your notifications once they're sent.

## Weekly, monthly and courses

Keywords: weekly, monthly, weekly events, monthly events, repeating events, repeat, repeats, every week, every month, recurring, series, run club, book club, next one, next date, course, sessions, class pass, drop-in, drop in
Open your event and set Repeats, under More options, to Every week, Every two weeks or Every month. Every month keeps the day, like the first Sunday of each month. The morning after each date, the next one appears with the same title, place, price, spots and look, and guests who open last week's link see the next date and can reply right there. To make each date yourself, turn off Make the next one automatically and use Make the next one; to stop, set a Last date or set Repeats to Just once. Tell Potto "6-week pottery course" or "8 sessions" and it's one event with every date on it, like "6 Wednesdays, Jan 6 to Feb 10"; it ends after the last session, and Run it again starts the next one. Say "$20 a class or $70 for 4 classes" for a pass and guests see "$20 a class, or $70 for 4", with a Pay button for each. "$10 a drop-in" reads "$10 a class". On Home and your page, the dates of one series share a card with "+N more dates".

## Kudos and reviews

Keywords: kudos, reviews, review, feedback, thank you, rating, after the event
The morning after your event, each guest who was in gets one message asking for kudos, one tap to send. You see kudos on the event after it ends, and they show on your page. Reviews never show a guest's email or phone number.

## Past events and the scrapbook

Keywords: past events, scrapbook, import, partiful, luma, eventbrite, portfolio, old events, history, csv
On your page, tap Bring in past events. Drop links from Partiful, Luma, Eventbrite or any event page, upload a CSV, or add one by hand. Potto turns each into a draft card you can edit or remove before adding. Street addresses are never stored, only the neighborhood or city.

## Talks and speaking

Keywords: talk, talks, speaking, keynote, webinar, podcast, panel, speaker
Talks live in your past events. Tap Bring in past events, then Add a talk, or drop a YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify, Apple Podcasts or Zoom link. Add the topic, organizer, recording and slides, and they show on your page under Speaking.

## The shop and pre-orders

Keywords: shop, sell, pre-order, preorder, orders, pickup, cake, merch, products
On your page, tap 🛍 Shop (on a computer it says Shop and bookings). In the Shop tab, list what you're selling with a price and how many you can make, and add pickup times. People order with a pickup time. In the Orders tab you see each order, set it to Paid, Ready or Picked up, and tap Text everyone when it's ready.

## Brand requests and quotes

Keywords: brand, brands, booking, book, request, quote, company, team event, offsite, corporate
Brands can request you from your page. Requests show when you tap 🛍 Shop on your page, under Team bookings. Tap Send quote with your price and terms; the brand accepts from their own page. Potto reminds you if a request has no quote after 4 hours.

## Brand onboarding

Keywords: onboard, onboarding, packages, vendor, menu, pdf, gmail, what brands can book
Open Set up with Potto and answer a few questions; skip any you like. Add a PDF, an email or a link and Potto drafts packages. Nothing goes on your page until you tap Looks good.
[Set up with Potto](/onboard)

## Discover and the events directory

Keywords: discover, directory, events page, find, nearby, found, listed, /events, not showing
Discover lists hosts and the events they chose to show there. Your events show only if they're set to On my page and Discover and your page has a name, a line about you and a face or photo. The same public events are listed at /events.
[Discover](/discover) [Events](/events)

## Privacy

Keywords: privacy, private, data, address, email, phone, safe, who sees
Guests' emails and phone numbers are only for you; other guests and the public never see them. Exact home addresses go only to guests who are in. Imported past events keep only the neighborhood or city.

## Deleting your account or an event

Keywords: delete account, close account, delete my account, delete event, cancel event, erase, delete everything
There's no delete button for your account or a whole event yet. Talk to a person and we'll do it for you. To hide an event now, set it to Link only in Guest rules.

## Removing guests and past events

Keywords: delete, remove, remove guest, remove past event, take down, unpublish, hide
To remove a guest, open the event's guest list and tap Remove. Past events on your page each have Edit, then Remove. To take an event off your page and Discover, set it to Link only in Guest rules.

## Talking to a person

Keywords: person, human, support, help, contact, team, someone, agent, talk
Tap Talk to a person in this panel. Say what's wrong; we attach the page you're on so you don't have to explain. We'll reply here and by email, usually within a day.
