# Pottery Lab help

Potto's help guide. Potto answers from this page only, so keep every line true to the product. Each `##` section is one answer: the first lines are what Potto says, so lead with the steps. `Keywords:` and `Questions:` help Potto find it (questions are split by `|`). Links become one-tap buttons under the answer: a page link, `/@{handle}` (the host's own page), or an app action: `#do:share` (open the share sheet), `#do:copy` (copy the invite), `#do:preview` (see what guests see), `#do:guests` (open the guest list), `#do:open` (open the event), `#do:new` (start a new event).

## Signing up

Keywords: sign up, signup, start, create account, register, new account, start your page
Questions: How do I sign up? | How do I make an account? | How do I start my page?
Go to Start your page, add your name, email, city and a password, then tap Create my page. Hosting friends is free. You land on your home screen, ready to make your first event.
[Start your page](/signup?mode=friends)

## Host codes and invite-only

Keywords: host code, invite-only, invite code, need a code, no code, get a code, ABCD, your turn
Questions: I don't have a host code. How do I get one? | How do I get a host code? | My host code doesn't work
Hosting is invite-only for now, so you need a host code from a host you know. Been a guest at an event here? Tap Your turn? on that invite. You don't need a code. No code? Tap join the waitlist on the sign-up form. Type your code in the Host code box; it says who invited you when it works. A code that doesn't work may be used up, so ask your host for a fresh one.
[Join the waitlist](/signup#ask)

Keywords: waitlist, wait list, waiting list, on the waitlist, room, when can i host
Questions: I'm on the waitlist. What now? | When will I get off the waitlist?
On the waitlist, we email you when there's room. You can skip it: any host you know can send you a host code, then you sign up with it.
[I have a host code](/signup)

Keywords: give code, my codes, my host codes, give host code, invite a friend to host, share code, waitlist people, codes for friends

Every host gets host codes for friends. Open one of your events, tap Your followers in Potto's panel, and they're under Your host codes. People waiting for a code show there too. Host an event with 3 or more guests and you get 10 more.

## Password rules

Keywords: password rules, characters, too short, strong password, length
Questions: What are the password rules? | How long does my password need to be?
Your password needs 10 or more characters. Anything works: words, spaces, numbers. Pick one you don't use anywhere else.

Sign in with the email and password you signed up with. "That email and password don't match" means one of them is off, so try Forgot your password. After 10 wrong tries we pause sign-in for a while; take a breather and try again later.

## What a page is

Keywords: what is a page, host page, my page, @, public page
Questions: What is a page? | Where is my page?
Your page is your public home, at a link that ends in /@yourhandle. It shows what you host next, your past events, kudos from guests and, if you want, a shop. People follow it with just an email, so they hear first next time. Tap My page in the menu to see it.
[Open my page](/@{handle})

## Choosing or changing your handle

Keywords: handle, username, change handle, rename, slug, @name, page link
Questions: How do I choose or change my handle? | Can I change my page link?
Your handle is the end of your page link, 2 to 30 lowercase letters, numbers or dashes. If you typed a club or group name at sign-up, it became your handle. Otherwise open an event, tap Your followers in Potto's panel, pick a handle and tap Create my host page. There's no button to change it later yet, so talk to a person and we'll change it for you.

Questions: How do I edit my event? | How do I change the date or time? | I put the wrong place
On Home, tap the event, then tap Edit details. Change the name, date, time, place, price or spots and tap Save details. The invite link stays the same, and guests see the change when they open it. To change who can come or who sees it, tap the guest rules line instead.

## Cancelling an event

Keywords: cancel event, call off, called off, cancel, postpone, not happening, rain out
Questions: How do I cancel my event? | I need to call off my event | Can I postpone my event?
There's no Cancel button yet. Tell your guests in your group chat first. On the event, turn off Remind guests the day before so no reminder goes out, and set it to Link only in Guest rules so new people can't find it. Then talk to a person and we'll call it off for you. To move it instead, tap Edit details and change the date.

Events are free unless you add a price. Open the event, tap Edit details, and type a price in Price per spot, or Pay what you like; leave it empty for free. Before you share a paid event, the last step asks how guests pay, so add a payment link or pick At the door.

## How guests pay

Keywords: pay, payment, pay link, venmo, paypal, cash app, paid spots, money, at the door, collect
Questions: How do guests pay? | How do I get paid? | Does Pottery Lab take the money?
Add a payment link (Venmo, PayPal.me, Cash App or any https link) when the wizard asks How do guests pay?, or later in Edit details under Payment link. Venmo, PayPal.me and Cash App links get the amount filled in for guests. Pottery Lab doesn't take the money; it goes straight to you. Guests can tap I paid, and you confirm it on their name.

Guests see the title, date, time, place, price and what to bring. For a get-together at home, guests see the neighborhood, and the exact address only once they're in. The guest list shows only guests who chose to show their name, and you can hide it in Guest rules. Guests never see other guests' emails or phone numbers.

Potto reminds guests who said yes the day before, by email, and by text when they gave a phone and texting is set up. The Remind guests the day before switch on your event is on by default; turn it off for that event if you'd rather not. You get a note in your notifications once they're sent.

## Weekly, monthly and courses

Keywords: weekly, monthly, weekly events, monthly events, repeating events, repeat, every week, every month, recurring, run club, book club, next one, next date, course, sessions, class pass, drop-in, drop in
Questions: How do weekly events work? | Does my monthly book club make the next one? | How do I set up a 6 week course? | How do I make my event repeat?
Open your event and set Repeats, under More options, to Every week, Every two weeks or Every month. The morning after each date, the next one appears with the same title, place, price, spots and look, and guests on last week's link see the next date. To stop, set a Last date or set Repeats to Just once. For a course, tell Potto "6-week pottery course" or "8 sessions": it's one event with every date on it. Say "$20 a class or $70 for 4 classes" for a pass.

## Series and episode numbers

Keywords: series, episode, episodes, episode number, hack night, changelog, #14, numbering, episodes before

Events that repeat, or share a name, are a series, and each date gets a number like "Hack night #14". Ran it before Pottery Lab? Open the event's Slots, series and channels and set Episodes before Pottery Lab (13 makes the next one #14). Add Demos shown and Games played under This episode, and they go in the Series changelog on your page.

## I'm in if a friend is

Keywords: i'm in if, in if, if my friend, only if, if sam, maybe if, friend is going
Questions: How does I'm in if a friend is work? | Can I say I'm in only if my friend goes?
A guest replies Maybe, then opens I'm in if a friend is and types a friend's name. When someone with that name replies I'm in, the Maybe turns into I'm in, or the waitlist if it's full. The friend is never told.

## Come with me

Keywords: come with me, bring a friend, hold a spot, friend's spot, plus one link, together
Questions: How does Come with me work? | How do I bring a friend? | Can I hold a spot for a friend?
After you reply I'm in, tap Come with me? to make a link for one friend. On most events their spot is held for 48 hours. On pairs and groups they join yours. Ask to join events still ask: the link holds nothing. Once you're both in, Who's coming says "Mia and Jo are in".

On your event, turn on Open table and add 2 or 3 questions. Guests who are in answer them, and you see suggested tables of about 4 under Guests; friends who came together stay together. Once you confirm, each guest sees "You're at table 2 with Ada and Lin".

Within a day after each event, guests who came get one "Same table next time?" note. One tap replies I'm in to your next date, or holds their spot until you make it. Your second-RSVP rate (of the people who came, how many replied I'm in again) is under Guests and After.

## The shop and pre-orders

Keywords: shop, sell, pre-order, preorder, orders, pickup, cake, merch, products, order form
Questions: How do I set up my shop? | How do pre-orders work? | Where do I see orders?
On your page, tap 🛍 Shop (on a computer it says Shop and bookings). In the Shop tab, list what you're selling with a price and how many you can make, and add pickup times. People order with a pickup time. In the Orders tab you see each order, set it to Paid, Ready or Picked up, and tap Text everyone when it's ready. For a pre-order on one event, tell Potto what you're selling, the price, how many and when to order by: guests pick items when they reply, and orders close on the order-by day or when it sells out.

## Deleting your account or an event

Keywords: delete account, close account, delete my account, delete event, erase, delete everything, delete my data, remove my data
Questions: How do I delete my account? | How do I delete an event? | Can you delete my data?
There's no delete button for your account or a whole event yet. Talk to a person and we'll do it for you, your data included. To hide an event now, set it to Link only in Guest rules.
[Talk to a person](#person)

## Removing guests and past events

Keywords: remove guest, remove past event, take down, unpublish, hide event, kick out
Questions: How do I remove a guest? | How do I remove a past event from my page?
To remove a guest, open the event's guest list and tap Remove. Past events on your page each have Edit, then Remove. To take an event off your page and Discover, set it to Link only in Guest rules.
[See guests](#do:guests)

## Talking to a person

Keywords: talk to a person, human, support, contact, someone, real person, email you
Questions: Can I talk to a person? | How do I contact support?
Tap Talk to a person in this panel. Say what's wrong, or nothing at all: we attach your chat with Potto, the page you're on and your event, so you don't have to explain. A person replies here and by email.
[Talk to a person](#person)
