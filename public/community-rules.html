Community rules

Pottery Lab is for hosting and going to things with people you like. Keep it kind, keep it real, keep it safe.

The rules

  • Be kind. No hate, harassment, threats or bullying, about anyone.
  • No sexual content, and nothing violent or graphic.
  • No spam, scams, or fake events, hosts or kudos.
  • Don't share someone's private info: phone numbers, addresses, emails or photos they didn't agree to.
  • Events are real and safe. Say what guests should know: the place, the cost, who it's for.
  • Kudos and reviews are about events you went to.

We don't allow objectionable content or abusive people. Breaking these rules gets content taken down, and can get an account removed.

Report or block

  • Report: tap ⋯ on a post, photo, kudos or person, or long press a photo. On a host's page, use "Report this page" at the bottom.
  • Block: from the same ⋯, or "Block this host". You stop seeing their posts, photos and events. Undo it in Settings.
  • We look at every report within 24 hours. If three people report the same thing, it's hidden right away while we look.
  • The person you report isn't told who reported them.
