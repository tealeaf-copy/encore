Founding hosts

A small group of hosts who take the first paid team bookings before launch, and tell us plainly how they compare with their own events.

What you commit to

  • A few dates you hold for team bookings over the next three months.
  • Logging your prep hours, so brands see what custom work really takes.
  • Two honest numbers about your own public events: what they earn and how long they take.
  • One question after each booking: would you send your next brand inquiry through Pottery Lab?

What you get

  • The first paid brand bookings, some as paid pilots.
  • Brands who book you again pay a smaller booking fee, so repeat work stays here.
  • Your followers, yours to download at any time.
  • Guests from similar hosts who asked to hear about you, invited with their OK.
