01 · The night

One good night takes a week.

  1. MON

    You post a date in the group chat. Eleven people react with a heart. Two reply.

  2. WED

    You ask again who's in. Someone wants to bring a friend. Someone asks for the address.

  3. FRI

    You buy the groceries and borrow four chairs from next door. You send the address again.

  4. SAT

    It's a lovely night. You do the dishes after everyone leaves.

  5. SUN

    You send nine payment requests and feel odd about each one. Someone texts "we have to do that again". So you start over.

02 · The problem

Most of the work happens where nobody sees it.

Supper clubs, run crews, pottery classes, hack nights and a baker's Saturday drop all have someone like this behind them. Most host after a day job, and many pay for everything up front.

The thanks scrolls away in the group chat. Most event tools were made to sell tickets to strangers, not for someone who hosts the same twelve people every month.

03 · For hosts

What it does for hosts.

So we took that week apart. This is what Pottery Lab does for hosts today.

  1. 01 · Easier

    Less admin.

    • Write one sentence and Potto drafts the invite. Guests reply I'm in without an account and get a reminder the day before.
    • Every event has its own group chat. Spots, a waitlist and Ask to join keep the table the size you want. When a spot has a price, guests pay through your own pay link.
  2. 02 · Rewarded

    Every night you host counts.

    • The morning after, guests send kudos. Each one adds to your hosting record, with streaks and badges on your page that show the work you put in.
    • Guests can also leave a private note on what to try next. Only you see it, so you get a little better at your craft each time.
  3. 03 · A following

    Guests become regulars.

    • The kind words guests write stay on your page, so new people see what a night with you is like.
    • Anyone can follow you in one tap. Start a fan club, and members get your new events and drops first, with a chat of their own.
  4. 04 · Found

    New people find you.

    • List an event and it shows on the feed, on Discover and on the map.
    • Brands planning a team night find you by mood and occasion and ask to book. You accept, counter or decline.

05 · Community rules

What we promise each other.

  1. Trust and quality over quantity. Eight people you'll see again beat eighty you won't. Hosts make the night, and guests send kudos and an honest note, so every night makes the next one better for all of us.

  2. Safety first. Your events are private unless you list them, and only guests who are in get the exact address. You can report or block wherever you see something, and we look at every report within 24 hours.

  3. Made for you. Pick what you're into and follow the hosts you like, and your feed shows those first. Potto drafts invites and sends reminders, so you spend less time planning and more with the people you love.
