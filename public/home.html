How it works

From friends, to regulars, to bookings.

1

Host for friends

Plan it in one sentence. Encore makes the invite, collects RSVPs and sends the reminders, and Pip handles the follow-ups, so you can just host.

2

Build your page

Every gathering adds to your page: the photos, the kudos, who came back. Followers hear about the next one first, and supporters can chip in.

3

Get booked

Brands looking for something better than another happy hour find you, request a date and pay one invoice. You set the price and say yes to what fits.
