How it works

You bring the idea. Pip handles the rest.

1

Check who's in

Share a "would you come?" page. Pip counts the yeses, finds the best date, and calls it when enough people are in.

2

Welcome the right people

Say who it's for and approve each guest. A group chat opens the moment you say yes. Sell seats or a pandan cake if you like.

3

Feel the love after

Guests send kudos and tips the morning after. Your page fills with what people said, and they follow you for the next one.

Pip: "Four people are in for Saturday. Two more and it's on. Want me to nudge the maybes?"
