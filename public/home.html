About Pottery Lab

Host memorable experiences, with your fav people.

We've been the planner. This is the tool we wished we had.

Pottery Lab is where you host small events for your people, like a supper club, a game or hack night, a run club or a drop, and keep every one of them on your page.

Who it's for

For people who would rather meet eight than eighty.

Hosts

You already bring people together: a monthly dinner, a Thursday game night, a Saturday run. You want it to feel considered, without a week of group texts.

Guests

Friends of the host, and people they trust. You get one clear invite, reply in a tap, and know who else is coming.

New in town

Looking for a table that fits, not a crowd. Find hosts near you, follow the ones you like, and hear about their next event first.

How it works

Three steps, and the second one is your guests'.

01 · Host

Write a sentence

Say what you're planning. Potto drafts the invite with the time, place and spots, and it's live.

02 · Guests

They reply, no account

What makes it different

Calm by design.

  • Calm, not loud

    No countdowns, no flashing flyers, no ads. An invite says what, when, where and who's coming, and then gets out of the way.

  • Small on purpose

    Made for six to thirty people. Spots, a waitlist and Ask to join let you keep a table the size you want.

  • Tables that come back

    A series keeps the same people coming back: a monthly supper, a weekly run. Your regulars hear first.

  • Your own brand

    Your page gets your fonts, colours and logo. Shared places like the feed keep one calm frame, so every host reads well.

Trust

Your people, kept private.

Private unless you say so

An invite is seen by people with the link. It shows on the feed and Discover only when you list it. Guests reply with a name, not an account.

Report and block

Report a page, an event or a message from where you see it. We look at every report within 24 hours. Block someone and you won't see their posts, photos or events.

No ads

We don't sell ads or your guest list. Read the privacy policy, the community rules and how we handle security.
