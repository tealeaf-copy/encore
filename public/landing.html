How it works

Four steps, from picking accounts to the 90-day readout

Pick the accounts

Name the accounts the event should move before you pick a venue. Encore checks there are enough of them and that sales agrees.

Fill the room

The guest list starts from your CRM. AEs nominate from a link. Invites go out in the AE's or exec's voice, after they approve.

Run it

Venue shortlist, a budget built line by line with the assumptions shown, run of show, launch copy and a booth plan for conferences.

Follow up and measure

Each AE gets a drafted follow-up per guest the next morning. After 90 days, Encore compares invited accounts with the ones you held out.

Measurement

A result you can take to finance, including when the answer is no

Encore holds out a random group of eligible accounts before invites go out, then compares meetings and opportunities. If the difference is too small to read, it says so instead of rounding up.

Before day 90 you see meetings booked, follow-up rate and target accounts in the room, from the morning after.

  • Tells you up front if you have enough accounts. At 15 dinners a year, one program may not produce a readable test. Encore says how many accounts you need and pools by format across cities.
  • Three pipeline buckets with fixed definitions. Created after the event, existing deals with attendees, and meetings. Deals matched by company name alone stay out of the totals.
  • Your top accounts are never held out. Your CRO signs the account split before it's locked.
  • A written decision RevOps can rerun. Fund more, cut or test again, with the rule that triggered it and a CSV of every account.

Questions

What buyers ask first

Does this replace our events person?

No. Someone senior still owns the room, the venue relationships and the conversations with sales. Encore takes the list building, chasing, drafting and reporting, so the same person can run more events.

Does it replace Goldcast, Bizzabo or Luma?

No. Keep the platform you use for registration and streaming. Encore does the work around it: which accounts to invite, the plan and budget, AE follow-up, and whether the event was worth it.

We only run 10 to 15 events a year. Will the measurement work?

Maybe not on its own. Encore calculates how many accounts a readable test needs before you start. Below that, it pools results by format across cities and quarters, or reports results without calling them lift.

How do the budget estimates work?

Every line is a formula you can see and change: guests times cost per head times a city cost index, booth square feet times the rate, and so on. They're planning assumptions until you replace them with quotes, and the app shows the range.

What do we need to connect to start?

Nothing. Describe your next event in a sentence or pick a template. Connect your CRM when you want guest lists from your accounts and pipeline from your deals.

Will invites sound like they came from marketing?

Invites are drafted in the voice of the AE or exec who knows the guest, and they approve the final copy. Call notes help decide who to invite and never appear in what a guest reads.
