Privacy Policy

Last updated Nov 25, 2026

This page says what {{LEGAL_NAME}} ("Pottery Lab", "we") keeps when you host or reply to an event, why, and who sees it. It's written in plain English. If something here is unclear, ask us.

What we keep about hosts

  • Your account: your name, email and password. The password is stored as a scrypt hash, never in plain text.
  • Your page: what you put on it, like your page name, link, city, area, photo, tagline, photos of past events, and payment handles (Venmo, PayPal, Cash App) if you add them.
  • Your events: titles, dates, times, places, prices, questions for guests, notes, and the guest list.
  • What Pottery Lab needs to run: who invited you (host codes), followers, kudos, bookings from brands, and a log of changes to your events.

What we keep about guests

  • Your reply: your name, your answer (I'm in, Maybe, Can't go), and when you replied.
  • A way to reach you, if you give one: an email or a phone number.
  • Anything else you choose to tell the host: food needs, access notes, answers to the host's questions, a ride, a photo.
  • Kudos: what you loved about an event and a short note, if you write one.
  • Follows: your email, if you follow a host.

Why we keep it

To run the event: show the host who's coming, hold your spot, send the reminders and updates you'd expect, and let you change your reply. To build the host's page and scrapbook. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for ads.

Who sees what

  • Hosts see their own guests: replies, contact details, food and access notes, and answers. A host never sees another host's guest list.
  • Other guests see only first names (or names, if you chose to show yourself) of people who are in. They never see your email, phone or notes.
  • Public pages and the /events directory show only public fields: the event's title, date, area, price and the host's page. Exact addresses stay hidden until the host lets you in, if the host set it up that way.
  • Reviews and kudos show your first name only.
  • Contact details are scrubbed from anything public: emails and phone numbers are removed before a page is sent to a browser.
  • Brands see the details of their own booking. They never see a host's guests.

Texts and emails

If you give an email or phone, you may get messages about events you replied to: a confirmation, a reminder the day before, a note when the host lets you in or a spot opens, and the next morning, a one-tap kudos ask. Followers get a note when a host they follow announces an event.

  • Texts: reply STOP to any text to stop them. Reply START to get them again.
  • Emails: every guest and follower email has an unsubscribe link. One tap turns them off for that address.

Emails go out through the sending service the host or Pottery Lab set up (Resend, Postmark or SendGrid). Texts go out through Twilio. Address search uses Photon, an OpenStreetMap service, which receives what you type in a place field.

AI

Potto, the helper in Pottery Lab, uses Anthropic's Claude API. When a host asks Potto for help (drafting an invite, planning an event), the details they gave it are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Potto doesn't message guests or brands on its own.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in, and a second small one that only says you're signed in, so pages know to show your menu. No ad or tracking cookies. Your browser also keeps a private key for your reply (in local storage), so only you can change it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account or the event exists. Hosts can delete events and remove guests at any time, and can export or delete their whole account in Setup. Guests can change their reply to Can't go at any time. To have your details removed from an event, or your account deleted, ask us and we'll do it.

Children

Pottery Lab is not for children. You must be 13 or older to reply to an event or make a page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
