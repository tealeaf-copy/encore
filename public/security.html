Privacy and security

What Pottery Lab keeps, who sees it, and what we never do

Written for hosts deciding whether to bring their people here, and for guests who just got an invite. If something you need isn't here, ask us.

If you're a guest

  • No account needed to reply. You give a name and, if you like, a way to reach you. Contact details are optional. Your reply counts without them.
  • The host sees what you share with them: your reply, contact details, food needs, and answers to their questions. Other guests don't see your contact details or answers.
  • Only you can change your reply. Your browser holds a private key for it. Nobody else can edit or cancel it.
  • Pottery Lab doesn't message you on its own. Invites and updates go out when the host sends them, by the channel you chose.

If you're a host

  • Your guest list is yours. Export it as a CSV any time, including answers to your questions.
  • Public numbers are totals only. Your page can show turnout (how many came, how many came back) for sponsors and venues. Guests' names are never shown in it.
  • Brands see what you choose to show. A booking shares the details of that booking and the agreement for it, nothing about your guests.

How it's protected

ControlHow it works today
Sign-inPasswords are stored as scrypt hashes, never in plain text. Sessions are signed and can be revoked on the server. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited.
DataOne service with its own database. Each host's data sits in its own workspace, and every read and write is checked against who is asking.
Invite pagesGuests' pages only receive the fields meant for guests. Everything else is removed on the server before it's sent.
Browser protectionsEvery page runs under a strict content-security policy, so a page or an upload can't load code from elsewhere.
UploadsLimited to images, PDFs, video and plain data files, and served so they can't run code.

AI

When you ask Potto for help (planning an event, writing copy, a gift plan), the details you gave it are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the answer. The gift planner also searches the web for public news about the company. Potto doesn't send anything to guests or brands on its own.

Deleting things

Hosts can delete events and remove guests. To delete your account, or a guest's details from an event you went to, ask us and we'll do it.
