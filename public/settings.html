Brand insights

Brands on Pottery Lab can see what kinds of events people love, as totals only, for example "supper clubs in Brooklyn, 40+ people, 88% would go again". Your activity is left out of those totals unless you choose to count it. If you do, your replies, kudos and interests are always mixed with at least 9 other people and 3 events, and never carry your name, email or anything that points to you. Brands can't download it.
