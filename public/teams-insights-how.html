Brand insights

How insights work

Brands use Pottery Lab to find hosts and learn what kinds of experiences their buyers would love. Insights help with that, without anyone being watched. Here is exactly what brands can and can't see.

The rules

  1. Only people who chose to be counted

    Your activity is left out of brand insights unless you choose to be counted, in Settings under Brand insights. Everyone starts left out. You can change your mind any time, and the next totals leave you out again.

  2. Totals, and only big enough ones

    A number shows only when at least 10 different people and 3 different events are behind it. Anything smaller says "Not enough yet". If hiding one cell would let someone work it out from the others, we hide the next smallest too. Counts of people show in bands, like "40+", never exact.

  3. No names, emails or profiles

    Brands never see who went to what, who said what, or anyone's contact details. The data behind a total carries no names, emails or handles. Hosts are named, because their pages are public, and their numbers follow the same rules.

  4. Nothing to download or sell

    There is no export of guest data in insights, and we don't sell it. The only list a brand can export is its own guest list, and only the people on it who said yes to sharing their details with that brand.

  5. The summary is written from the totals

    The short "What we'd try for you" summary is written from the same totals on the page: kinds of experience, percentages and prices. No one's details go into it.

What goes into the numbers

Counted, as totals

  • Which kinds of events people went to, and when, where and at what price
  • Whether guests said they'd go again in their kudos
  • Whether people came back to the same host
  • Interests you picked in "What are you into?", and what you do, if you added it in Settings
  • How teams rated their bookings

Never shown to brands

  • Your name, email, phone or page
  • Which events you went to
  • What you wrote in kudos or messages
  • Your exact location
  • Anything about one person, even without a name

Fill rate uses the headcount a host's invite already shows to everyone, so it doesn't read anyone's reply.

A brand's own events

When a brand invites you to an event it booked, you choose whether to share your name and email with that brand. What you say about the event afterwards is counted into themes like "Meeting people", once 10 or more people have answered. A brand only quotes you if you tick "OK to quote me", and then only by first name.
