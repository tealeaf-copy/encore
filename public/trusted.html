How a host becomes Trusted

The badge is earned on Pottery Lab, never bought. We check it every day, and a host loses it the day they stop meeting it.

The rules

    What Trusted hosts can do

    They can turn on Instant book, so an open date is yours as soon as the deposit goes through. Everyone else reviews each request first.

    Checks on every host

      Covered on every booking

      • The full price for your group before you pay, with every line shown.
      • A deposit holds the date. It's charged only when the host accepts.
      • If the host cancels, you get everything back and we help you find another host.
      • Messages stay on Pottery Lab. Emails, numbers and addresses are shared once you're booked.
      • Reviews come only from teams who booked and guests who came.
