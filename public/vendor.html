Set up Pottery Lab as a vendor

Everything procurement and finance usually ask for, in one place. You set up one vendor, Pottery Lab, and book any host or maker through it.

How it works

Who you contract with
Pottery Lab. Hosts and makers are paid by Pottery Lab; you never onboard them one by one.
Paying
Card when you confirm, or an invoice paid against your PO within 30 days.
Cancelling
Each host's own terms, shown before you book. Where a host has none: free up to 7 days before, half the quote inside 7 days.
If a host can't make it
A vetted replacement or a full refund, your choice.
Quotes
All-in, fixed for 14 days, downloadable as a PDF to attach to a PO.
Insurance
Each host's certificate is shown on their page when it's on file. Where it isn't, we get one before you pay, and can add your company as additional insured.
Gift address lists
Used only to make and ship that order, shared only with the maker making it, and deleted 30 days after delivery.

The vendor kit

W-9, a certificate of insurance, our standard terms, and a completed vendor questionnaire if you send us yours. We email it within one business day.
